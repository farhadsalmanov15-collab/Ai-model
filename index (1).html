This is a visual estimate of cosmetic damage only. It can’t judge battery health, internal faults, or whether the phone works.

The model was trained on a small set of photos (about 70) using Google Teachable Machine, and it only separates intact from broken phones. The score is the model’s confidence that the phone is intact, and the grade bands (Poor below 40, Fair 40–69, Good 70–89, Excellent 90 and above) are set by hand, not learned.
